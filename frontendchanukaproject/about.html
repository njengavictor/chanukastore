Empowering Student Hustlers & Local Business Vendors

Chanuka Market Store is a local e-commerce platform designed to remove barriers for student entrepreneurs, small business vendors, and everyday shoppers across Rongai, Gataka, and Eldoret.

We saw that small business owners and student vendors often struggled with high courier delivery fees, middleman cuts, and distrust in online transactions. Chanuka provides a low-commission marketplace backed by escrow vault security and verified local pickup points.

Platform Guidelines & Verification

Review how we handle escrow transactions, delivery pickups, and buyer/seller protections.

  • ✓ M-Pesa Express Payment: Fast and direct STK Push payments linked directly to verified seller accounts.
  • 🛡️ Vault Security: Buyer funds are held safely in the Chanuka Escrow Vault until delivery is confirmed.
  • 💰 5% Platform Fee: Sellers retain 95% net payout on all successful sales.
  • 📍 Cooperative on Campus: Centralized drop-off and pickup point for university students.
  • 📍 Gataka Stations: Pickup available at Gataka - Destiny and G-place Gataka.
  • 📍 Eldoret Station: Zion Mall pickup hub.
  • 📍 Gataka Delivery: Ksh 150 local delivery option within Gataka.
  • ⚠️ Station Inspection: Buyers inspect items at the pickup station prior to releasing escrow funds.
  • ⚠️ Dispute Window: Damaged or incorrect items can be flagged instantly to hold payout.
  • ✓ Full Refund Guarantee: Unfulfilled orders receive a 100% refund back to the buyer's M-Pesa number.
