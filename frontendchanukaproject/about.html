ABOUT CHANUKA MARKET • BEI YAKO, BEI POA

Connecting Student Entrepreneurs, Local Vendors & Everyday Shoppers

Chanuka Market Store was built with one purpose: make local commerce safe and affordable. We saw an opportunity to build a platform where entrepreneur students and small business vendors can sell their products online and reach a wider online market without having to pay high courier fees this will enable them grow their business. So we built a marketplace that works for us.

We connect student hustlers, small business entrepreneurs, electronics shops, fashion vendors, and even well established businesses to buyers looking for a variety of goods at local prices - electronics, fashion, furniture, groceries, household, liquor & drinks. No middlemen. Just verified pickup stations and escrow protection.

🎓 Students
Turn side hustle to business. List in 2 mins, zero fees.
🏪 Vendors
Keep 95% of every sale. Only 5% flat fee.
🛒 Buyers
Variety of goods, local prices, escrow safety.

Our Mission

To empower local economy by making online selling safe, affordable and accessible to every student and vendor.
