Legal Policies & Operational Terms

Transparency and compliance guidelines governing buying, selling, data protection, and cookie usage.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy details how Chanuka Market Store collects and protects your data:

  • Information We Collect: We collect details necessary for processing orders and buyer-seller chat routing, including your name, phone number, and chosen delivery pickup point.
  • M-Pesa Checkout Data: Payment details are securely submitted directly via M-Pesa STK Push. Chanuka Market Store does not store your private M-Pesa PIN.
  • Data Confidentiality: Your contact information is strictly shared with the assigned product seller to facilitate pickup and order fulfillment. We do not sell user data.

By browsing or creating a shop on Chanuka Market Store, you agree to the following Terms of Service:

  • Escrow Vault Protection: Buyer payments are held securely in escrow until order delivery and verification at the designated pickup point.
  • 5% Platform Fee: Sellers receive 95% net payout on all successfully fulfilled items. Platform fees are non-refundable once an order is complete.
  • Prohibited Items: Sellers are strictly forbidden from listing illegal goods, hazardous materials, or fraudulent products. Offending accounts are suspended immediately.

Chanuka Market Store uses cookies and local storage to enhance your browsing experience:

  • Essential Cookies: Required to keep you logged into your Seller Dashboard and save items in your active shopping cart.
  • Preference Cookies: Remembers your preferred pickup station (e.g., Cooperative on Campus, Gataka - Destiny) for faster checkout.
  • Managing Consent: You can manage or disable cookie preferences anytime through your browser settings or our Cookie Banner below.
