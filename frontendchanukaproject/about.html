How Escrow Safeguards You

Shopping locally shouldn't involve financial risk. Chanuka Market acts as a neutral third-party holding facility for funds during every transaction.

  • ✓ Buyer Deposits: Payment is sent securely via M-Pesa to our system vault upon checkout.
  • ✓ Verification Window: The buyer inspects the package at pick-up or upon door delivery.
  • ✓ Automated Payout: Once approved by the buyer, funds are released directly to the seller's M-Pesa account.
02

Seller Return & Refund Policy

We maintain clear standards to protect both merchant reputations and buyer confidence:

  • • 24-Hour Inspection: Buyers have 24 hours post-delivery to report inaccurate items or hidden defects.
  • • Faulty Goods: Items that do not match the listing description are eligible for a 100% full refund from escrow.
  • • Return Dispatch: Returned goods must be deposited at an official station in original condition.
