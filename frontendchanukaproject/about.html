Escrow Transaction Guarantee

How Chanuka Stores Process Payments

When you order an item using M-Pesa STK Push, your funds are **never disbursed to the seller immediately**. They enter the **Chanuka Escrow Vault** where they are securely protected.

Money is only transferred to the merchant after you receive, inspect, and confirm satisfaction with your item at your chosen pickup station.

Seamless Order Flow

1

M-Pesa STK Push Checkout

Select your preferred pickup station and authorize payment on your mobile phone.

2

Seller Order Dispatch

The seller receives an automated alert and delivers the order to your designated location.

3

Inspection & Payout

After inspecting your item, you confirm in chat and the escrow vault releases funds.

5% Transparent Fee

Chanuka Market retains a automatic 5% platform commission to support server maintenance, escrow handling, and direct support services.

95% Net Seller Payouts

Merchants retain 95% of their total sale price, disbursed straight to their registered M-Pesa line immediately upon buyer confirmation.

Seller Return Policies

Returns and exchange policies are determined according to individual seller guidelines. Buyers inspect goods at stations prior to release.
