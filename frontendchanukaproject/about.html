ABOUT CHANUKA MARKET

Connecting Student Hustlers, Local Vendors & Everyday Shoppers

Chanuka Market Store was built with one simple purpose: BEI YAKO, BEI POA. We saw students in Rongai, Gataka & Tuala struggling to sell their products online because of high delivery fees, lack of trust, and platforms that take 20-30% commission. So we built a local marketplace that actually works for us.

We connect student entrepreneurs, mama mboga, local shops, and small vendors directly to a variety of buyers looking for affordable goods - from electronics, fashion, furniture, groceries, to liquor & drinks. No middlemen, no expensive couriers. Just drop your product at our verified pickup stations and we handle the trust.

Turn your side hustle into a real business. List in 2 mins.
🏪 For Vendors
