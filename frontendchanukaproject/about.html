Marketplace Policies & Guidelines

Standard operational guidelines ensuring safety for buyers and sellers.

🛡️ Escrow & Buyer Protection Policy

All customer payments via M-Pesa STK push are held in the secure Chanuka Escrow Vault. Funds are only released to the seller (95% net payout) once the buyer inspects and confirms satisfaction upon delivery or pickup.

📦 Pickup & Station Drop-off SLA

Upon order placement, sellers receive instant notifications specifying the exact pickup station chosen by the buyer. Sellers are expected to drop off the item within 24 to 48 hours for buyer collection.

🚫 Prohibited Items & Vendor Conduct

Sellers must list genuine products with accurate pricing. Counterfeit goods, illegal contraband, weapons, or misleading descriptions will result in immediate account termination and escrow forfeitures.

🔄 Refunds & Dispute Resolution

If an item is damaged, misdescribed, or unfulfilled, buyers can request order cancellation before escrow release. Admin support will mediate disputes and process full refunds when applicable.
