How Chanuka Market Works

Your trusted student & campus marketplace operating with secure M-Pesa escrow protection, direct seller chat, and structured local pickup points.

Escrow Protection

100% Safe Payments & Store Operations

Chanuka Market acts as a secure intermediary between buyers and independent store owners. When you order an item using M-Pesa STK push, your funds do not go directly to the seller immediately.

Instead, funds are held safely in the Chanuka Escrow Vault. Money is released to the seller only after you confirm pickup or delivery at your designated station.

Order Workflow

  • 1 Buyer checks out via M-Pesa STK push and chooses a pickup station.
  • 2 Seller receives instant notification with buyer contact details and station.
  • 3 Item is dispatched to pickup location for buyer inspection.
  • 4 Buyer confirms satisfaction; escrow releases payout to seller.

Seller Earnings & Transparent 5% Fee

Selling on Chanuka Market is simple and fair. Sellers set their own item prices and receive 95% net payout on every transaction.

5% Platform Cut

Automatic platform maintenance fee deducted at checkout to keep server operations running seamlessly.

95% Seller Net

Disbursed straight to the seller's M-Pesa phone number upon successful delivery confirmation.

Direct Chat

In-app direct messaging between buyer and seller for smooth order coordination.

Seller Return Policies

Returns, exchanges, and refunds are determined strictly according to individual seller instructions and item descriptions.

Buyers are strongly encouraged to inspect items carefully at the pickup station prior to final confirmation, or communicate directly with the seller in private chat before purchase.

Official Pickup Destinations

Orders are delivered to standard designated stations during checkout:

  • Cooperative on campus
  • Gataka - Destiny
  • G-place Gataka
  • Eldoret - Zion mall
  • Doorstep Delivery within Gataka (Ksh 150)
