Empowering Student Hustlers & Local Business Vendors

Chanuka Market Store is a local e-commerce platform designed to remove barriers for student entrepreneurs, small business vendors, and everyday shoppers across Rongai, Gataka, and Eldoret.

We saw that small business owners and student vendors often struggled with high courier delivery fees, middleman cuts, and distrust in online transactions. Chanuka provides a low-commission marketplace backed by escrow vault security and verified local pickup points.

Platform Policies & Guidelines

Review how we handle escrow transactions, delivery pickups, and buyer/seller protections.

  • ✓ Vault Security: When a buyer pays via M-Pesa, funds enter the Chanuka Escrow Vault first.
  • ✓ 5% Platform Fee: Sellers receive 95% net payout automatically upon order confirmation.
  • ✓ No Monthly Charges: Listing products is completely free for all verified sellers.
  • 📍 Cooperative on Campus: Centralized drop-off and pickup point for students.
  • 📍 Gataka Stations: Pickup available at Gataka - Destiny and G-place Gataka.
  • 📍 Eldoret Station: Eldoret - Zion Mall pickup center.
  • 📍 Gataka Door Delivery: Flat Ksh 150 delivery fee option within Gataka.
  • ⚠️ Inspection at Pickup: Buyers must inspect items at the pickup station before confirming receipt.
  • ⚠️ Dispute Window: If an item is defective or incorrect, buyers can flag it before escrow release.
  • ⚠️ Refund Guarantee: Valid unfulfilled or incorrect orders are 100% refunded to the buyer's M-Pesa.
